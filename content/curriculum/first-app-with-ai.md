The gap between "AI wrote me some code" and "there is a thing on the internet that I made" is where most people stall. This session closes it. We build one small, genuinely useful app together and put it online with a real URL before the end of class.

The app we build: **a Show Your Work page** — a personal site that pulls from something you already generate (your GitHub activity, a folder of images, a notes directory) and turns it into a living page of what you are making. It is small enough to finish, real enough to keep, and it is yours the moment class ends.

## Who this is for
People who can describe what they want but have never shipped it. Some prior exposure to AI chat tools helps. No prior deployment experience assumed — the part where it goes live is the part we do most carefully.

## How the 90 minutes go
- **Spec first (15 min).** How to describe an app to an agent so it builds the right thing. This is the skill that matters most and the one most people skip.
- **First working version (30 min).** Get something running locally, fast and ugly. We talk about why ugly-and-running beats elegant-and-theoretical.
- **Make it yours (25 min).** Swap in your own data source, change the look, add the one feature you actually care about.
- **Ship it (20 min).** A real deploy to a real URL. What "deployed" actually means, what it costs (nothing, here), and how to update it later.

Then 30 minutes of Q&A where we fix whatever broke — because something always breaks, and watching it get fixed is most of the education.

## What you'll leave with
A deployed app at a URL you can share, the repository behind it, and a repeatable process you can point at your next idea. Plus the recording.

## What you need before class
A machine you can install software on, a GitHub account (free), and the setup from AI OS — or any working coding agent. If you have not taken AI OS, arrive 15 minutes early and we will get you running.
