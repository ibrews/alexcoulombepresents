title: MR venue concept & Stage Presence prototyping
date: 2026-07-28
summary: The most detailed venue concept yet -- a soundstage where guests bring a film world to life -- plus what Stage Presence already supports.

## Notes

- Venue premise: guests are on a Hollywood soundstage and accidentally bring a film world to life; they must resolve the chaos they've caused -- comedic tone inside a world that takes itself very seriously
- Flow: themed queue with old cameras and scenic flats, three gateway modules teaching core interactions, a magical transition where a painted backdrop gains depth and comes alive, then into the main venue (forest, palace, underground, town zones)
- Exit: a judgment scene, then a reentry module with a highlight reel, profile badges, and a physical "banishment decree" takeaway
- Design philosophy -- "augmentation first": painted scenic backdrops are a reliable underpainting; AR adds depth on top, with a knowing wink that guests are technically on a set
- Stage Presence already supports: co-location, avatar teleport/switching, grab/scale/share/throw physics, lightweight "reality capsule" player outlines, and controller-as-object-tracker for precise moments
- Key MR advantage over pure VR: physical objects don't need to be tracked to be touched -- tracking is only needed for puzzle-solving logic
- Prototyping plan: Phase 1 uses only tables, chairs, and found objects (framed as a "parallel dimension" where familiar objects behave differently once you look through the headset); custom set pieces wait for Phase 2
- Onboarding must clearly teach the world's rules early, or later interactions feel frustrating instead of discoverable
