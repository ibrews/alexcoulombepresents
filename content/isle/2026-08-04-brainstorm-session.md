title: Body jumping emerges as the lead mechanic
date: 2026-08-04
summary: Open ideation on VR-unique mechanics, converging on body jumping, plus precise MR/VR/spatial-skinning definitions.

## Notes

- Body jumping (piloting and hopping between avatars) emerged as the strongest mechanic -- already in Stage Presence, culturally trending, and fits the soundstage premise ("the actors have become puppets")
- Tradeoff: co-located body jumping is third-person puppeteering (keeps spatial relationships, limits movement); fully virtual is full embodiment (can fly, breaks the real-world anchor) -- likely a role for one player at a time given the cognitive load
- Other mechanics discussed: an apple-throwing/time-manipulation co-op game, classic theater games (pass-the-energy-ball, zip-zap-zop) as onboarding with VR twists, rescaling/cloning/time-slowing as VR-unique interactions
- Reality-mode definitions clarified precisely: mixed reality = passthrough with digital overlays; spatial skinning = the real world is transformed but physical objects are still directly engaged; full VR now seen as a tool for "space multiplication" -- brief VR travel sequences let one physical room stand in for many MR-skinned locations
- Ideas raised: redirected walking through the same physical rooms with different skins; theater-style physical set changes between visits; modular rooms with built-in mechanical pulleys to swap elements without staff
- Strong repeated advice: don't over-scale the first demo -- start small (1-2 modules), prove it, then raise to build out further
- Aesthetic direction floated: a unifying tactile look across the venue (Rec Room as a model), possibly even a cardboard "brown boxing" prototype aesthetic that could become a charming permanent look
