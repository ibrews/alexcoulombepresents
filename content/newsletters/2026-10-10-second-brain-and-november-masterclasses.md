## A second brain you can actually use

A lot of my AI workflow depends on a knowledge base: recipes, lessons learned, project memory, and ways to check whether the work actually succeeded. Teaching the prompts alone only gets us so far. I want you to have a useful starting point of your own.

That's the idea behind **ACP Second Brain**, a curated collection of reusable workflows that members can use with their own AI setup. It draws on how I work, with company and personal information removed.

**The first course kit is an early pilot.** You'll practice:

- Giving an assistant useful project memory, so a fresh session can pick up where you left off.
- Finding a recipe and producing an output you can verify.
- Stopping a bounded job, saving a checkpoint, and resuming it safely.

The exercises use a fictional project on your own computer. You'll need Python 3.11 or newer; Unreal, a headset, and paid AI API access are optional for this first kit. There's a local search connector for compatible assistants, plus a manual reading path.

**Current ACP members get this pilot at no additional charge, across all membership tiers.** A purchase of either November AI masterclass also includes access. Use the email attached to your membership or class purchase to sign in.

[Open the Second Brain course kit →](https://alexcoulombepresents.com/members/second-brain)

I'd love to learn where it helps and where you get stuck. **Pilot feedback is optional and off by default.** If you opt in, you can submit structured results, review what you're sending, and later export or delete that feedback. The feedback form doesn't collect your prompts, project files, or conversations. You can use the kit without sharing feedback.

Over time, I want this to grow into a much broader companion for creative work: Unreal workflows, UOXR, packaging for TestFlight, SceneAudit, and video QA. Those are future additions; this first kit starts with the foundations that make the rest dependable.
