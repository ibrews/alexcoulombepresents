## Deep Dive with Lumen for UE 5.8, with Sean Spitzer

I'll say this plainly: Sean was one of my bosses at Epic Games, on the team that creates Epic's educational content. He's one of the sharpest people I know at explaining how Unreal actually works, and he's brilliant at it.

- Side-by-side comparisons, and the settings that matter most

If you've ever wondered whether you can afford Lumen on your target hardware, this is the class.
